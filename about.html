About

Rooted in North Carolina. Built for how real businesses run.

Root and Ridge Systems is a founder-led firm based in Asheboro. We help owner-led businesses get their systems working together, so their people can focus on the work that matters.

Our name

Why "Root and Ridge"

The root is everything underneath: the software, the steps and the connections that keep a business running. Most people never see it, but everything depends on it.

The ridge is what the world sees: how quickly you reply, how clearly you communicate, how smoothly a job goes from first call to final invoice.

When the roots are healthy, the ridge stands strong. That's the work we do.
