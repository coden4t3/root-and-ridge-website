How it works

We learn how your business works, then build around it.

Four steps, clear prices, and nothing built until you've approved the plan.

  1. Discovery Call

    Free · 20 minutes · phone or video

    You tell us what your business does, which tools you use, and what's slowing you down. We'll share a few quick wins and let you know honestly whether a Root Review makes sense.

  2. Root Review

    Fixed fee · about 1–2 weeks

    We follow real work through your business, from first contact to final payment, and look at every tool and handoff along the way.

    What we need from you

    • A working session with you and whoever runs the office
    • A look at the tools you use today

    What you get

    • A friction map and ranked recommendations
    • New tool costs, if any, and a fixed Build price

  3. Build

    Fixed price · about 2–4 weeks

    Once you approve the plan, we set everything up in your accounts, test it with real jobs and customers, and train your team. Half is due to start and half at launch.

  4. Care

    Optional · monthly

    Thirty days after launch we review what's working together. If you'd like us to keep watch, Care covers monitoring, fixes and small improvements, month to month.

At the end

What you own when we're done

No black boxes. Everything we build is yours and stays understandable.

  • Every automation, running in accounts that belong to you
  • A plain-English guide for each one
  • A short recorded training your team can rewatch
  • A list of every tool involved and what it costs

Questions about the process

How much of my time does this take?

Plan on about two hours during the Root Review, mostly one working session and a walkthrough of the results. During the Build we'll need short check-ins and about an hour for training.

Do you work on site?

Yes. In and around Asheboro and across much of North Carolina we're glad to come to you. We also work remotely by screen share.

What do you need access to?

Only the accounts involved in the work, and only for as long as we need them. We use your own logins or invited user accounts, never shared passwords sent by email.

What if I just want the plan?

That's fine. The Root Review is yours to keep. You can build it yourself, hand it to your team, or hire us later.

Tell us what's slowing you down.

Start with a free 20-minute Discovery Call. We'll ask how your work flows today, point out a few quick wins, and tell you honestly whether a Root Review is worth it for you.
