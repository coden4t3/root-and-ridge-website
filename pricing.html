Pricing questions

Why charge for the Root Review?

Because it's real work and a real deliverable: a full review of your systems and a plan you can use with or without us. If you hire us to build it within 30 days, the full $950 is credited to the Build.

What if I don't want the Build?

No problem. The plan is yours to keep and use however you like.

Will I need to buy new software?

Sometimes, but we always try what you already have first. Any new tool is listed in your Review with its monthly cost before you decide. You subscribe in your own name and pay the vendor directly, with no markup from us.

How do payments work?

The Root Review is paid up front. Builds are 50% to start and 50% at launch. Care is billed monthly and you can cancel with 30 days' notice.

What decides the Build price?

The number of automations, how many tools are involved, and whether any data needs to move. You'll get a fixed price in writing before any work starts.
