Questions

Common questions

Do I have to switch software?

Usually not. We start with the tools you already pay for and connect them. We only recommend a new tool when the ones you have can't do the job, and we'll show you its monthly cost before you decide.

I don't know much about automation or AI. Is that a problem?

Not at all. You know how your business runs; that's the part we need. We explain everything in plain English and you approve every change before we build it.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Everything is set up in your own accounts, documented, and handed over. If you ever stop working with us, it keeps running.

What if something breaks later?

Every build includes testing and a fix window after launch. For ongoing peace of mind, our optional monthly Care plan covers monitoring and fixes.

What does it cost?

The Discovery Call is free. Everything after it is fixed-price and published on our pricing page, and the Root Review fee is credited in full if you hire us to build what it recommends.
