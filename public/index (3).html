Privacy policy

Last updated October 3, 2026

This policy explains what information Root and Ridge Systems ("we," "us") collects through rootandridgesystems.com and how we use it.

Information you give us

When you fill out our contact form or book a call, we collect the details you choose to share, such as your name, business name, email address, phone number and message. We use this information only to respond to you, schedule conversations, and provide services you ask for.

Service providers

We use trusted third-party services to run this website and handle form submissions and scheduling, such as our web host, a form-processing service and a booking calendar. These providers process information on our behalf and only as needed to provide their service.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If we add privacy-friendly analytics in the future to understand which pages are useful, we will update this policy.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We do not add you to marketing lists without your permission.

Client systems and data

When we work inside a client's software accounts, we access only what the work requires and follow the confidentiality terms in our client agreement.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you've sent us at any time by emailing hello@rootandridgesystems.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
